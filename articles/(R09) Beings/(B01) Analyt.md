## SGEN Analyt

Ah, Analyt! He’s a complex fella. See, he has unparalleled loyalty to the regime. No, seriously, he’s one of The Collectors. Yet he’s different. Well, I mean everyone Is different, but he’s very different. Different from the other Collectors at the very least. See, he wants freedom. The constant battle between the forces of good and evil, balance and imbalance, order and chaos.. it’s too much for one person. He wants to end it, yet he knows he can’t. And it crushes him from the inside. He does things by the book, with extreme accuracy and perfection. Yet why is he doing it? In recent times, he’s developed a sort of ‘screw you’ to the establishment. Even though he created said establishment but you know. It’s outgrown him. He wants changes, the other collectors don’t, however. They want to preserve the balance they’ve so delicately achieved. Millions of Forms lost and what for? For the world to crumble? Is that what Analyt wants? No, no he doesn’t. But what other choice does he have?

He’s simple. He sticks with his data and his mathmateics. Everything he does is caluclated, he doesn’t do anything uneccessary. But he doesn’t like that life anymore. He just wants to live alongside his people, not cursed to provide servitude for a cosmic being that doesn’t even care about him. But again, what can he do? He loves his people, and his partner. He’ll do anything for them. And if that means holding on to the torture for a little bit longer, then well, so be it. 

But all things must come to an end, right? Has he finally had enough? He has. He very much has. You know I keep circling back to the idea of what can he do. But have you figured out an answer yet? I haven’t and that’s soul crushing for Analyt. At some point people just snap. And he’s been preventing that point for a long time. How much more numbers can one person take? Constantly seeing the souls he has to sacrifice for one last time. He feels no pleasure, I mean what pleasure is there sacraficing people? Dealing with energy they cannot predict.

You know he’s not cartoonishly evil, as some might suggest. You might even think so. But what would you do? He’s lived in State all his life, dealing with the paperwork from a young age. Just trying to keep the lights in The Foreground. He lost his parents early when the Layer they were analysing collapsed unexpectedly. He's been alone for most of his life, why do you think he fiercely protects those he loves?

I think you get the point, an overworked, tired being who just wants to sleep. But can’t because the next person in line won’t do a good enough job.
