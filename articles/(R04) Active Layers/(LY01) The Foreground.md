##### Description

The Foreground was once a beautiful green plains. Grass and flowers and trees. Nature's birthplace within The Corridor. Now it's the living space of thousands of beings. Beings who just want to live, with no complexities. No flying cubes, no shrinking roots. Just a flat plain with one use. Houses and shops occupy every corner, nature has lost it's dominant place. But don't worry, it's still there. Nature exists in other ways, weather, beneath your feet.

---

##### Roots

The Foreground was a gift from The Corridor to The Collection. There's nothing wrong with it, no inverted physics, no slowing time. Just a place where people can live.

---

##### Ruleset

One of the few Layers that do not have one. Nothing is wrong with this Layer, it's the most practical pocket of reality.
