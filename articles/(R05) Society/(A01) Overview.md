##### Persona

Society exists when the reality warping system exists just round the corner? Wow, I have to admit, I wasn't prepared for this. You know any sane person would just look at it and say "yeah.. double it and give it to the next person."

Everything in society revolves around your Room. If you're in Stock, enjoy an average life. Special? Unparalleled luxury but your merits are less known. And god forbid you're in Store. Yeah.. you're not apart of society at that point. See we've already established The Corridor rules the word. Everything must revolve around it. You'd think everyone Is running around with their heads lagging 10ft behind but no. Life just continues. Everyone lives, socialises. There is no sense of impending doom or world ending.

Don't worry, The Collection isn't as maniac as you think. They wouldn't let their dear population know that there is something below. They just think the regime is totalitarian, I mean The Collection would disagree but no one tell them that. The Forms just think people are being killed because fun or whatever. They don't about this. Well, I mean you do, So it's either you've been officially approved by The Collection. Alternatively, and this is my personal take, you're just here to stir up some trouble for our favourite regime. Be a nerdy rebel or whatever.

Well, Forms obviously know about The Corridor. It's pretty hard to explain why your year 4 teacher just fell through the floor of the ground he was stepping on. Can't be chalked up to science for once. But they only know that it exists, and sometimes it has an emotional breakdown that causes a new reality to be born. So dangerous, got it.

Now in more recent times, society has gotten a bit.. unconventional for The Collection. Not malicious, just fed up of the same daily sacrifice. Everyone's on edge y'know? Why do you think Actuality is being taken seriously by The Collection? Gotta appease them somehow. The Collection has been pleasing people all their existence. Society is continuing to live, do their assigned task, get food, team effort sure. But they want change. Something to happen. And The Collection has plenty of liabilities to make that happen.
