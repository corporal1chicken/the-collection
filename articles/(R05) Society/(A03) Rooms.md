# > A03 // Rooms

##### Persona

The social hierachy of the world. Once your class has been determined, you very rarely change. And if you do, you're doing something very right so keep at it!

How is your Room decided? Simple. It's entirely based of your family history. Better hope your ancestors did a good job and were loyal to The Collection.

Your room determines everything in this world. Your respect, task. Everything.

---

|ROOM|DESCRIPTION|
|---|---|
|State (ST)|Where the top officials of The Collection belong. They do not live in luxury, they are overworked and overwhelmed. They are constantly managing an emotional reality warping system. Of course they have no time to rest!|
|Special (SP)|Less important Collection officials but still highly regarded. They live in comfort, they do their tasks and have the most privilege outside their work. Yet they are often hidden away. The higher ups at State often get the credit for their work.|
|Spare (SA)|Friends and children of State officials. They do no task, you might say they're entirely useless. They live in ultra luxury, at the expense of State. State doesn't mind, as long as their children are comfortable, that's all that matters.|
|Service (SE)|Is this what you call the middle class? Higher? I don't know but they are respected by The Collection. These Forms provide a service that is irreplacable. They are valued, receive better care, more freedom and more priority from The Collection. Sometimes, if you're lucky, you can get hired by The Collection and ascend to SGEN.|
|Stock (SO)|The regular population. This is where the most people reside. And they just live. They have generic tasks, receive entertainment and sufficient resources. They're not stuck in Stock though, they can rise to Service or as equally lower to Secondless.|
|Secondless (SC)|You're at your final chance. Do not mess up. One last mistake and you'll be demoted to Store.|
|Store|Beings who are disgraced or criminals. They usually don’t have a task to do, and very isolated. You can redeem yourself and leave the Store category. The only way a person can is to sacrifice their family in the name of The Corridor. As a result, very few people have ever left. When The Collection requires power both to run their layers and to appease The Corridor, they often turn to those in Store to kill.|

---

##### Ending

And there you have there Rooms. The social class of the world. You can't really control it but you have to live with it.
