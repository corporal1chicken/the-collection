## Persona

It's not everyday you get judged by a reality warping system now is it? One wrong move and it's all over. No no like over over. You've heard that phrase a million times but seriously, do NOT make The Corridor mad. Its the one that judges your Humanity.

Humanity is one of the two metrics that actually matter in this world. It is judged directly by The Corridor and you're stuck with it till the age of 70. Half your fate is determined at the fine age of 7. I guess everything in this world is obsessed with the number 7 huh. Don't worry, I don't understand it too.

This metric matters deeply to The Collection. I mean as I said, half your fate is determined. It is vital to The Collection that they take advantage of the gifts your given - If you're lucky. If you've somehow altered your fate, and The Corridor decided that you're worth keeping, you gain the ultimate gift anyone would ask. Unlimted and safe travel to every single Layer made by The Corridor. And hey, it's benefical to The Collection too, you pretty much get forced to work for them. Where else can you find a person who makes the emotional machine calm? Nowhere! Nepotism at it's finest!

Even the Layers themselves recognise this gift, Sentient Layers often ignore you, and don't turn you into an > A02 // Amalgamations.

Now comes the part you're dreading to hear - what happens if you're not worthy to The Corridor? Nothing really. Its confusing, and even The Collection themselves don't understand. But hey at least you're safe, right? Right?
