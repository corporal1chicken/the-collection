# > A01 // Actuality

##### Persona

Out of the 3 metrics, this one is the most pointless. Blunt, sure, but the truth. Actuality is a made up metric created by the everyday beings. They want to move away from the rigidity of The Collection. Something more social and more open and free. Even some of the Partitions themselves, like Assurances and Agreement, persuaded The Collection to allow this to happen.

In practice, Actuality has no effect, Morality and Humanity are the ones that matter. Socially though? This is everything. Actuality is how the rest of society views you. You can either be respected highly or hated by everyone. The rules don't change though, The Collection sees right through it. But hey, maybe beings will address you by your title instead of your name. That sort of privilege. Not sought after by everyday people.

Now this was a calculated move by The Collection. They saw that the population was beginning to doubt them, even rebel against them. Bringing new ideas such as The Rite, a huge threat to the stability. The founders? High Actuality, finally people were beginning to express themselves in other ways. Ways that The Collection doesn't approve of.

However, in more recent news, The Collection has begun to take Actuality more seriously. They've even began using it internally to determine who gets sacrificed. Maybe they'll do the morally correct thing and make it official. Or they'll just keep going about their day, like they have been, with no regard for what the people want.

##### Ending

You want a conclusion? Actuality is pointless. That's it. It does sound cool though, at least I think so. But cool doesn't get you points in The Corridor. Action does, that's what The Collection may do.
