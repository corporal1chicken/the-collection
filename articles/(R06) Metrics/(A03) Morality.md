## Persona

Did you think morality referred to how kind you are? Incorrect. Sometimes you just can't be kind in this world, it's all about survival. Morality refers to how loyal you are to The Collection. The thing you need to remember is that The Collection IS moral. I mean do you think they want to kill hundreds per month? No. It was calculated, they did the maths. They are moral and they're the basis of it. How moral you are is how much you're willing to follow them. It all comes full circle!

Remember how your > // Humanity was judged at the age of 7? Well don't worry, The Collection gives you plenty of time to get your morality in order. They judge you at the age of 70, 63 years after your Humanity is judged. That's a long time to wait! Surely you wouldn't go crazy in that time right? Anxiously waiting to see if you'll get demoted to Secondless or instantly sacrificed. Yeah it's a lot, but don't worry! Your friends at the Partition of Agreement have the numbers, the data. A rising number of people are defecting before people get their Morality judged. Maybe you should be apart of that number, huh.

## Ending

Humanity and Morality go hand in hand. They're taken into account together, by everyone. That's the only thing The Collection cares about. Better spend those 63 years doing something useful. Maybe in the afterlife you'll finally be able to pass GCSE foundation maths.
