# > A05 // Objects

##### Persona

You would think objects are just objects right? Well the majority of them are just that.. depending on their Layer. Many objects simply exist, they're either manufactured or natural. Yet some objects from more unstable Layers tend to defy these established rules.

Objects also have thresholds. They can absorb the humanity and morality of people who use or touch them. Unlike Form thresholds, they have 2 outcomes. It's one or the other. No in-between..

---

##### Outcome 1

-- Requirements: High Morality

Simple. They no longer work as intended. It loves The Collection so much that it'd rather enjoy it's current state of loyalty. A door? Won't open. An electrical line? Emits electricity forever. Now some of these "frozen" states are useful to The Collection.. or they equally be as not.

---

##### Outcome 2

-- Requirements: High Humanity

It opens up its control to The Corridor. The Corridor controls it as a vessel of emotions, an expression of itself. Sometimes, even as a direct extension. The more objects within a single Layer have high humanity, the more likely it is that when the Layer destabilises, it'll become an Origin Layer.

---

##### Ending

Such complexities for such simple things. That's The Corridor for you, I guess. The Collection must monitor everything. Even down to the pen you left from your 3rd time resitting GCSE foundation maths.
