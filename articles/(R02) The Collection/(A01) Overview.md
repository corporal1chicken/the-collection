## Persona
The Collection's mission is simple; kill a few to save a million. Who are they killing a few for? The Corridor of course! The Collection's sole purpose is to appease The Corridor. Give it its soul quota and it's happy. And if it's happy then The Collection is happy. The well-being of everyone below The Collectors is irrelevant until the threat of world shattering emotional destruction has been mitigated. So, there you have it. The blunt nature of The Collection. They don't hide their true intentions, it's necessary for survival.

---
## Partitions
The Collection is large, it is complex, it requires subsections in order to keep running. Each Partition is led by 1 person, usually a Special official, and they answer directly to The Collectors. There are 7 Partitions:

        - Partition of Agreement
        - Partition of Adjustment
        - Partition of Assurances
        - Partition of Alliances
        - Partition of Allocation
        - Partition of Abandonment
        - Partition of Auditing

Every Partition has a role, every one has a single purpose. The Collection does not waste time on those who do not deserve it. Instead they allocate others to do it for them.
