## Persona
This is the peak of bureaucracy. If you hate rules, stay far away from Allocation. Though I guess if you do then you'll be stuck in your Room forever.

The Partition of Allocation handles travel within the Layers. They create the rules, they identify the hazards, the threats. They regulate travel, even down to travel within each Layer. If you're from State, Spare, Service and Stock, you're allowed to travel. Though you've got to apply for what they call a 'Transparency Pass'. This pass is the single key that allows you to travel. That's of course if you haven't been deemed worthy by The Corridor. If you have, well you pretty much get a free pathway in The Collection. You're useful, it means you won't die as quickly as the guy you're replacing.

By obtaining a 'Transparency Pass' you also waiver your legal right for The Collection to rescue you should the Layer you're travelling in collapses. Yikes. Signing away your legal right? Yeah but look The Collection rarely will abandon you. Do I have to remind you again that they do care about their people? They do, so it's not common they'll ignore you. There's dedicated Indentation teams that will come and help you, regardless of your Room.

Allocation is also the most collaborative, they frequently work with the Partition of > // Assurances and > // Alliances for security. See they do care! They providing you with an escort to ensure no savages take advantage of the subset.

## Ending
Want to travel? Pack your bags and prepare because you'll need. You and Allocation have no idea what will happen in the Layers. You can either peacefully walk through it or it'll violently destabilise and you've got a chance The Collection rescue. It's a gamble, but at least the ink isn't toxic.
