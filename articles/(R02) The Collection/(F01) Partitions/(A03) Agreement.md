## Persona
See the Partition of Agreement works differently to the other areas of The Collection. They actually want to serve the people, I mean, not like the others don't. No one Is as dedicated as Agreement. Hell it's literally their name!

This is the most well liked Partition of them all. They deal directly with the people. You have an issue? Ask Agreement. Missing resources? Agreement handles it. Beings would do anything for Agreement, they only do their task for them. Every bureaucracy needs a friendly face, right? Well they're not faking it, it's totally true.

Has anyone asked why they're called Agreement? Not really, because why ruin what's working? The Partition of Agreement is actually the only Partition that doesn't have some sort of heavily armed militia. Why would they need one? The people would riot for them! The commoners are essentially their army. Agreement actually convinced The Collection to start realising what the beings call > // Actuality. It keeps people happy, and if they're happy The Collection is also happy.. once The Corridor has been appeased.

Does Agreement like The Corridor? Hell no! Well as long as they don't tell the people, then they'll be fine.
