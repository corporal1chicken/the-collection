## Persona
Oh. Abandonment. By far the most corrupt Partition out of them all. Even The > // Collectors want nothing to do with them and just allow them to operate.

They are located in the > // The Playground, they practically own it. They perform and carry out scientific experiments there. They have fun with it, why do you think it's called the playground! Now their official purpose to find ways Indentations can stabilise Layers, but in more recent times they've begun to perform horrific experiments on Forms. Yet somehow, they always succeed. Eventually at least, even if they have to go through a thousand Forms, it always succeeds and no one can figure out how. Abandonment work in absolute secrecy, even some of the other Partitions do not know what they do.

Beings disappear everyday and the first suspect is always Abandonment. They are known to take Forms, sometimes willingly, sometimes forcefully and experiment on them. Sometimes, and don't tell anyone I said this, they "collaborate" with other Partitions for more.. willing participants.

---
## Ending
Simple, just avoid Abandonment at all costs. But just make sure that cost isn't your life. If you get pulled into their mess, say goodbye to life as you know it. Don't worry, The Collection will look, but it's not always they find you. So don't count on it.
