## Persona
What's a society if there's no crime? There has to be someone protecting the commoners right? Even if "protecting" them means stopping some petty theft..

The Partition of Assurances is one of the most well known Partitions by the everyday beings. They were the first to be set up by The Collection. They have one purpose and that is to protect people. They stop crimes, they deal with disputes and they're the first responders. Alright so multiple purposes but hey, they are well liked and respected, especially by The Collectors . They're simple beings, they don't cause The Collection any trouble (unlike some other Partitions that.. shall not be named..).

They operate within > // The Foreground primarily, offering their services to SGEN and SRON members. Yes. They do care about Secondless rooms. Why wouldn't they..? They're beings after all. Employees within the Partition of Assurances are dedicated individuals and they truly care about safety. A lot of them are employed almost every match! They also operate in other Layers such as > // The Playground, providing basic security and escorts to their employees.

Do they love The Corridor? No, not really. It makes their job harder. But hey, who even loves The Corridor? Surely there's no organised group trying to rebel against the syst- oh that's right. > L01 // The Circuits. The Partition of Assurances have had a lot of cases dealing with them. Crime rates have skyrocketed due to their influence. So much so that for the first time in a very long time has official Assurance enforcers have begun dying. They are struggling to find Forms to replace them.. and The Circuits are only getting stronger.
