## Persona
Who do you call when Assurances can't handle it? You call the Partition of Alliances. They're the heavy hitters. When you need urgent and deadly backup, you call these guys. In fact, you don't even need to call them, they sometimes just turn up. Just in case, redundancy sure. And probably because you would've needed them anyway.

The Partition of Alliances was the most recent Partition to be formed.. at least that's what The Collection claims. We all know they weren't but whatever, they're useful. They were formed to combat > // The Circuits, that's their sole goal. Rid The Corridor of these pathetic waste of beings. Sorry, these guys can get angry quick. They are former Assurance enforcers who believed they weren't doing much. Now they're helping their former allies in crushing the rebels.

These guys are a lot more equipped, you see these guys running down the street, then well they would suggest you go to your Room and stay there. These guys are no joke. There's been 4 major attempts to defeat The Circuits and sure, they've all failed. Yet The Circuits are still on the run. The latest group formed to defeat these guys were Faction Four. A refined, streamlined, fighting force.

Rumour has it The Collection is worried Faction Four is getting too advanced, and some of them were sent on a secret mission. No knows what but they haven't been seen in a few matches. Expect to see Alliance enforcers alongside Assurance ones. If you do, it means you're totally safe! Well, that's if you're a law abiding being. If not, well, say goodbye to what you know..
