## Persona
These guys are the masterminds behind The Collection. Well, masterminds to themselves, psychopaths to other and straight up serial kills to others. They have the whole variety!

Look, you've heard of cold hearted criminals. The ones that kill without purpose. The Collectors? No no. They did the maths. They sat down and calculated how they'll survive. The line "Kill a few to save millions" isn't out of nowhere. It is out of necessity. Are they justified? Arguably, but it's either be annihilated by a emotional tantrum driven system or kill those who don't agree. I wouldn't suggest disagreeing though, you'll be the first to be fed to The Corridor.

The Collectors come from the fallen entities that tried to previously befriend The Corridor. I guess now we know why The Corridor favours The Collection so much. Finally someone who understands it and isn't going to do anything stupid. The first thing The Collectors done when The Corridor allowed to let them exist alongside it was.. what? _Clears eyes_ Oh.. they sacrificed their family members first.. yikes.

Allegedly they made a promise to only do what they are doing out of necessity. I mean they are, are they not? Why do you think they let people out of Store for that exact reason?

## Ending
So there you have it. The Collectors. Cold and calculated. Not cold hearted murderers, just people who looked at the grim reality and decided what had to be done. No one else has a better plan so I guess they gained traction.
