
## Persona

Now how does The Collection such complex.. uh, things? Layers, that's it! Every single Layer has an on file Reaction. A list of steps, carefully crafted steps in what to do if a Layer destabilises. No, you don't run around like a headless chicken. The Collection has no time for silly jokes. What are they reacting to? The Layer's Ruleset of course! The Ruleset contains everything weird about a Layer. It's localised reality, a detailed log. If the Reaction is the checklist of what to do, the Ruleset is the checklist of what not to do!

---

## Reactions

We should've probably went through Rulesets first but whatever. Reactions is what The Collection uses in response to a Layer when it is destabilising. It is a highly coordinated, meticulously planned (probably untested) plan of what to do. It lists what Indentation teams will be used and what they would do. Reactions are given to every Layer. Regardless of whether The Collection thinks it'll destabilise or not.
