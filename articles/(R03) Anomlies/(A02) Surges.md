## Persona

Each Layer is classified into a Surge, they are groups of similar Layers that share the same emotion. Makes it easier for The Collection to mange. Now you only need to specialise in 1 emotion rather than 5!

Surges are purely based on what the Layer's emotion is, not really the actual content within it. There are a total of 5 Surges, 4 of them are split between 2 Partitions and the final one is directly controlled by Indentations.

---

|Name|Description|Emotions|
|---|---|---|
|Surge // Radiance|These Layers are happy, and if you’re happy then they’re happy. It's a mutual agreement. Don't be too happy though, otherwise the Layer gets too happy. And when the Layer gets too happy, it decides it doesn't want to actively exist. It'd rather enjoy it's current state of happiness for the rest of time. When it destabilises, it freezes itself, enjoying its life long happy state. But it will also freeze anyone and anything within the Layer so don't stay too long.|Happiness, Joy|
|Surge // Friction|Filled with uncontrollable rage, they do not feel, they just do. Layers of complex emotions. It will just exist, maintaining it's relatively peaceful state. Until someone or something ruin it. Why can't they just be in peace? When it destabilises, it will destroy and remove anyone and anything that it deems to be unruly. You don't know whether it'll be you. Sometimes it'll just destroy what The Corridor wants, rather than itself.|Angry, Rage|
|Surge // Fluctuation|Unpredictable, one moment it can be happy and the other moment angry. Just don’t question anything. Matter of fact, just don't do anything. Keep it balanced, compliant with itself. Do you want to be the one that destroys the perfect balance? Tip the scales? When it destabilises, it yearns to make everything equal. Balanced as all things should be. But it takes it to the extreme, for everything one thing, it'll add a thing. What that thing is? Your guess is as good as mine. It'll take items from nearby Layers and place it within its own.|Unease, Depressive|
|Surge // Tremor|Often hidden and tucked away, it’ll rather just exist. It's as simple as that. As long as you exist alongside them and do not try to change its state, it’ll reciprocate. It will allow you to exist as well. But when you try to do something? You're changing it's state. You altering it. But what affects it? See that's the neat part, you don't know. When they destabilise, it puts the blame on itself and begins to tear itself apart, highly localised but threatening to the other Layers.|Scared, Adrenal|
|Surge // Incompatibility|The issue is the surroundings. Not itself, not you but where it is. As long as you change the scenery, it'll change itself to match. When they destabilise, they rapidly change the environment to fit its own criteria.|Disgust, Rejection|
