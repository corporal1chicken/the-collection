## Persona

Now stay with me here, explaining Layers are complex. Layers are physical manifestations of The Corridor's thoughts and emotions in a localised space. Each Layer, depending on how they were manifested, have their own independent mind. Sometimes, The Corridor still controls it long after it is created. Still with me? Good. Each have twisted rules that affects its local reality. One can rapidly age you just because it felt like it. Another can be quiet for a hundred years and suddenly wake up and violently cry itself back to sleep.

These Layers are the biggest threat to The Collection. This is what it was made for, cleaning up the physical mess of The Corridor. Layers are created annually. Some are stable, some are even helpful. But most are violent, most disrupt the natural order to things. The Collection has came up with many ways to identify, group and control them.

Layers exist physically, oh did I mention they can exist within each other? The ground you're walking on could contain a Layer that can suddenly destabilise with no obvious cause. One wrong move and you can be the reason why 200 Forms lose their lives. Don't worry, there's no consequences if that's what you're worried about.
