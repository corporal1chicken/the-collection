## Persona
Before we start, I would like you to take comfort in that fact you do not know what The Corridor is. No, seriously, The Corridor? The bane our of existence. It's both the reason why we survived and the reason why we haven't been wiped off the face of the planet.

So, you're a brave one, huh? Well fine. Let me enlighten your life, with knowledge about The Corridor. Stay away from it. Do not express any emotion. Otherwise it'll use it against you, in fact, it'll use it against everyone and everything. See, it creates these things The Collection calls 'Layers'. They are manifestations of The Corridor's emotions. How does a non living thing have emotions? Don't mention it, like at all. I don't know, The Collection certaintly doesn't.

The thing you need to understand is; The Corridor is everything. Our society must revolve around it. Our life must as well. One bad action, one bad misstep and like I said, our world and your world, we can say goodbye too. Just do what The Collection says. Let them handle it. Whatever mess The Corridor creates, just let the experts deal with it. Don't attack the monster head on.
