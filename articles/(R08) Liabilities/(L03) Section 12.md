# > L03 // Section 12

##### Persona

Oh, look what we have here. If you thought the Partitions were doing a flawless job keeping everyone in the dark, think again. Eventually secrecy finds its way to the surface. Literally.

Way out in the freezing, miserable mountains of Siberia, the surface dwellers decided to do what humans do best: dig into things they shouldn't. They started drilling down to analyse the Earth’s core, and surprise, surprise, they’ve gotten entirely too close to stumbling into the nightmare that is The Corridor. Don't worry, feel free to blame who you call the Soviets. You surface beings love to point the blame at everyone else, just like us!

They don't know exactly what they're looking at yet. But they suspect something massive is down there, I think massive is quite an understand, don't you think? Courtesy of the Partition of ------- who have informed us that the Soviets created a group specifically to figure out what the hell is going on. They've named it "Section 12".

This is the ultimate nightmare scenario for The Collection. Absolute discovery. -------'s entire mission was to monitor the above world. I mean they didn't fail, The Collection didn't exactly tell them what to do if someone figured out. So I guess it's unfair to blame them. Nonetheless, The Collection is worried. Very worried. They know what the surface beings are capable of, and they’d rather their problems stay up them.

The Corridor is completely unpredictable. It reacts violently to people simply travelling between regular Layers. How do you think this unfeeling, morality free machine is going to react when an entirely separate, unsanctioned civilisation drills into its core? It needs human souls just to stay stable, if it gets disturbed by Section 12, the violent emotional outburst could easily wipe out the world below _and_ the world above.
