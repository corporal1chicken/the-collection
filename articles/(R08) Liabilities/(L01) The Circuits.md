# > L01 // The Circuits

##### Persona

These guys? Ugh, fine. I'll talk about these clowns. They should be renamed to "The Circus" causing problems for my beloved The Collection. No no, form your own opinions first.

They are the single largest faction sitting outside The Collection. And their mission? Simple. They quite literally want to rewire our entire world from the inside out. What does that mean? You tell me, they want to tear down The Collection, and they want to destroy The Corridor. The Collection likes to pretend they have everything under control, but off the record? The Circuits have already infiltrated every single Partition we have. They're everywhere. They are your friends, your family, even the neighbours you wave to in The Foreground. Right now, they’re playing a massive game of catch-up, trying to match the system's power, and they are getting dangerously good at it.

Have you ever thought about death? You know in an ageless world. You don't get the liberty of a natural death, oh no, that's the easy way out. And The Circuits know that all too well. They are masters of firearms and ranged weapons. Let that sink in for a second. In a world where you can only die at the hands of others, now someone can do it without you looking? Yeah, they're feared. Hell, even The Collection had to form dedicated paramilitary groups to stop them. They're growing, and growing rapidly.

Where do they get their recruits? The bottom of the barrel, obviously. Most of their members crawl out from the Store rooms. The people who have been completely isolated by the system. They are the ones who have absolutely nothing left to lose. They aren't just mindless, just violent thugs, though the political propaganda they are spewing is actually starting to work. People in the lower Rooms are listening, credit where it's due.
